# Review

This node is for you; no agent runs here. A card arrives once its mockup is built.

## Look at it

1. Open the card. In its chat, find the latest Mockup run and choose **Preview** beside its saved `index.html`. **Open in new tab** shows the page full-window; narrow the window to see it on a phone.
2. Read Mockup notes for what is a placeholder and what a real build would need.
3. Skim the Design brief's palette, adjectives and hero beside the page.

## Check

- **Is it this business?** Name, town, offer, hours and address match Research, and nothing invented reads as fact.
- **Is it the owner's taste?** It should look like their shop and their posts, evolved, not like a template with their name on it.
- **Does it lead somewhere?** One clear primary action, visible without scrolling on a phone.
- **Would you show it to the owner?** Copy that sounds like them; no fake reviews; the "Concept homepage" line in the footer.

## Decide

- **approved** → Done. If you host the page somewhere, paste its address in Mockup URL first.
- **rework** → Brief. First write what to change in the card's hand-off notes, under a line such as `Review feedback:`, and be concrete: "warmer palette, closer to the shopfront", "lead with cake orders, not the café", "the hero art reads as a blank box". Brief revises the brief from your notes, Mockup rebuilds the page, and the card comes back here. Feedback about the page only, not the brief, still goes through rework: Brief passes it on to Mockup unchanged.

If the facts themselves are wrong, correct Research or Website in the editor before choosing rework.
