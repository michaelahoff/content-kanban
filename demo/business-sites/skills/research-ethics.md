# Research ethics

This flow researches small businesses and the people who run them, to pitch them a better website. Collect only what the business has chosen to make public, and use it only in its business context. The owner may read everything you write.

## Sources to use

- The business's own website, menus, price lists, booking pages and posts.
- Its listings: Google Business, Yelp, TripAdvisor, its Facebook page, booking platforms.
- Press and local media about the business; chamber of commerce, downtown association and award pages.
- A state business registry only to confirm the business exists or its trading name, never to look up people.

## People

- Name an owner or principal only when the business or the press states it in a business context ("co-owners Jane Doe and John Roe opened…"). Cite it with the source's year: ownership changes.
- Name staff only as the business presents them, such as a team page or stylist profiles.
- Use a person's professional profile (LinkedIn, a work account) only when it names this business or the business links to it. If you are not sure it is the same person, say so in notes and leave it out of the fields.

## Never collect, never infer

Home addresses, personal phone numbers and emails, family members, age, health, relationships, politics, religion, personal social accounts, court, credit or property records about people, and photos of people beyond what the business itself posts.

## Reviews

Summarize themes with ratings and counts per platform. Do not quote or name reviewers, and do not repeat a reviewer's accusation as fact.

## Honesty about uncertainty

- Every fact carries its source URL. "Not found" is better than a guess.
- Give dates when sources disagree or may be stale: "Hours per the Google listing, read 2026-10-17; the website still shows summer hours."
- Mark inferences as inferences: "Colors read from the site's CSS", "Logo described from the storefront photo".
- If you cannot tell whether a profile, page or person belongs to this business, leave it out and say why.

## Conduct

- Never log in, get around a paywall, login wall or rate limit, or use a copy of something you were not meant to see.
- Fetch politely: the pages you need, not a crawl of the site.
- Never contact the business or anyone connected to it.
