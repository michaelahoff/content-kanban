# Review: your pass before recording

No agent runs here. Work through the card, then choose a route from the card editor.

- **Title.** Is the `RECOMMENDED:` title (or a better one from the list) the one you would click? Make it the card title.
- **Thumbnail.** Compare Codex's images and Claude's `thumb-a.png` and `thumb-b.png` in the card chat at phone size. Put the winner in the gallery and make it the display image.
- **Intro.** Read it aloud. Under 45 seconds? Does the first line pay off the thumbnail?
- **Script.** Fill every `[PROOF: …]`, `[DEMO: …]` and `[CREDIBILITY: …]` placeholder (the notes list them). Cut anything you would not say.
- **Copying.** Nothing of the outlier's title wording, thumbnail text and layout, or script lines.

## Routes

- **approved** → Done, when you are ready to record.
- **revise** → Titles, which runs Titles, Intro, both thumbnail nodes and Script again. First write what to change in the hand-off notes, starting with `revise:`, for example `revise: titles too generic, keep #4; script, cut chapter 3`.

To redo one part only, drag the card to that node instead: it runs from there down to Review.
